Getting Started

New to SecureWave?

SecureWave is a multi-platform VPN service that encrypts your internet connection and protects your online privacy. It supports WireGuard, OpenVPN, and IKEv2 protocols across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.
1. Create a free account on our website. 2. Download the SecureWave app for your platform from the Downloads page. 3. Sign in with your account credentials. 4. Select a server location and tap Connect. Your connection is now encrypted and secure.
SecureWave is available on Windows 10+, macOS 12+, Ubuntu/Debian/Fedora Linux, iOS 15+, and Android 8+. The number of simultaneous devices depends on your subscription plan.

Technical

Protocols, features, and privacy

SecureWave supports three industry-standard protocols: WireGuard (recommended, fastest and most modern), OpenVPN (widely compatible, TCP/UDP), and IKEv2/IPsec (excellent for mobile, handles network switching well). You can select your preferred protocol in Settings.
The kill switch blocks all internet traffic if your VPN connection drops unexpectedly. This prevents your real IP address and unencrypted data from being exposed during brief disconnections. Available on Pro and Enterprise plans.
No. SecureWave operates a strict no-log policy. We do not record your browsing activity, connection timestamps, bandwidth usage, or DNS queries. Our infrastructure is designed so that even we cannot see what you do online. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Split tunneling lets you choose which apps or websites use the VPN tunnel and which connect directly to the internet. This is useful when you need VPN protection for sensitive activities while allowing local network access for things like printers or local streaming services.
Troubleshooting

Common issues and solutions

Try these steps: 1) Check your internet connection without VPN. 2) Try a different server location. 3) Switch protocols in Settings (WireGuard to OpenVPN or vice versa). 4) Restart the SecureWave app. 5) Check the System Status page for any ongoing issues. If the problem persists, run a diagnostic from the app and contact support.
VPN speed depends on several factors: 1) Connect to a server closer to your physical location for lower latency. 2) Use WireGuard protocol for the fastest speeds. 3) Avoid servers with high load (check server status in the app). 4) Test your base internet speed without VPN to rule out ISP issues. 5) If on Wi-Fi, try a wired connection.
Some websites block known VPN IP addresses. Try: 1) Switch to a different server in the same region. 2) Try a different server region. 3) Use split tunneling to exclude that specific website from the VPN tunnel. 4) Clear your browser cookies and cache. If the issue persists, contact support with the website URL.
Run our built-in DNS Leak Test to verify. If a leak is detected: 1) Enable the kill switch in Settings. 2) Set DNS to "Default" or "Cloudflare" in Settings instead of "Custom". 3) On Linux, ensure systemd-resolved is configured correctly. SecureWave automatically configures DNS leak protection on most platforms.
