How It Works

How we protect you

Three layers of defense that work together to keep your data safe.

1. Encrypted Tunnel

Your traffic is wrapped in AES-256 encryption using the WireGuard protocol, creating a secure tunnel between your device and our servers.

2. Threat Detection

Our AI-powered system inspects traffic metadata in real time, identifying threats before they reach your device using behavioral analysis.

3. Adaptive Response

When threats are detected, the system automatically reroutes traffic, blocks malicious endpoints, and adjusts encryption parameters in real time.
