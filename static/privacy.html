Privacy

Privacy explained in plain language.

SecureWave is designed to keep the account portal focused on essential account data while the app handles the protected network session. That separation reduces unnecessary overlap and keeps the service easier to understand.

Account data

SecureWave stores the information required to authenticate your account, manage plan status, and support access to the dashboard and downloads.

Service operations

Operational telemetry may be used to keep the service functioning reliably, diagnose failures, and maintain product quality.

Support and recovery

When you request support, diagnostic context may be used to understand the issue and recommend the correct next step.

Browser-local preferences

Website display preferences and cached session cues are stored locally in your browser and do not represent VPN configuration on the device.
