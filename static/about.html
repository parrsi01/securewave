About Us

Building a More Private Internet

We believe everyone deserves online privacy. SecureWave was founded to make enterprise-grade security accessible, transparent, and effortless for individuals and organizations worldwide.

Our Mission

Privacy Is a Right, Not a Privilege

At SecureWave, we are committed to defending digital privacy as a fundamental human right. Our mission is to provide fast, reliable, and transparent VPN infrastructure that empowers users to take control of their online presence.

We believe the internet should be open, free, and safe for everyone. That means no activity logging, no data brokering, and no compromises on encryption standards. Every architectural decision we make is guided by one principle: your data belongs to you.

Whether you are a journalist operating under censorship, a remote worker securing sensitive communications, or an everyday user who values discretion, SecureWave delivers military-grade protection without complexity.

Our Values

What Drives Us Forward

Three core principles guide every product decision, engineering choice, and customer interaction at SecureWave.

Privacy First

Zero-log architecture from day one. We cannot share what we do not collect. Our infrastructure is designed so that even we cannot see your browsing activity, connection timestamps, or bandwidth usage.

Transparency

Open documentation, honest performance metrics, and clear communication. We publish regular transparency reports, explain our technical decisions publicly, and never hide behind vague marketing claims.

Innovation

Continuous improvement powered by cutting-edge research. From adaptive route optimization to next-generation WireGuard tunneling, we invest heavily in engineering to deliver measurably faster and more secure connections.

Our Approach

Built by Security Engineers

SecureWave is developed by a small team focused on building reliable, transparent VPN infrastructure. We prioritize working software over marketing claims.

Open Standards

We use well-audited, open-source protocols (WireGuard, OpenVPN, IKEv2) rather than proprietary solutions. No security through obscurity.

Honest Claims

We only advertise features that are actually deployed and working. Our server count, protocol support, and platform status are always up to date.

Growing Infrastructure

We currently operate servers in Germany (Frankfurt and Nuremberg) and are actively expanding to new regions based on user demand.

Engineering

Our Technology

SecureWave combines proven cryptographic standards with intelligent network optimization to deliver a VPN experience that is both secure and fast.

Server Health Monitoring

Our backend continuously monitors server health, latency, and load across all VPN endpoints. When a server shows degraded performance, the app automatically suggests healthier alternatives so your traffic takes the fastest available path.

Anomaly Detection and Threat Prevention

Machine learning models trained on anonymized network patterns detect unusual connection behavior, potential DDoS attempts, and protocol-level attacks in real time. When anomalies are identified, traffic is automatically rerouted through clean paths, maintaining both security and performance without user intervention.

WireGuard Protocol Integration

SecureWave is built on the WireGuard protocol, a modern tunneling solution with a minimal attack surface and state-of-the-art cryptographic primitives including Curve25519, ChaCha20, and BLAKE2s. Our implementation supports native platform integration on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Server Infrastructure

Servers in Frankfurt and Nuremberg running hardened Linux with WireGuard, OpenVPN, and IKEv2. Additional regions are being deployed as demand grows. All servers run on Hetzner dedicated infrastructure in Germany.
