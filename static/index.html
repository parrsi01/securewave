Protocols

Three Protocols, One App

SecureWave supports industry-standard VPN protocols so you can choose the best fit for your needs.

🛡

WireGuard

Modern, lightweight protocol with minimal attack surface. Uses ChaCha20 and Curve25519 for fast, secure connections with low overhead.

🔐

OpenVPN

Battle-tested and widely compatible. Supports TCP and UDP modes for reliable connectivity across restrictive networks and firewalls.

📱

IKEv2/IPsec

Ideal for mobile devices. Handles network switching seamlessly, reconnecting automatically when you move between Wi-Fi and cellular.
