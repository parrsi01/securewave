Before you choose

What does the price include?

Free includes 5 GB of VPN transfer each month and one device. Premium includes unlimited transfer and up to five devices. Both use the same available WireGuard app.

How does annual billing work?

The annual Premium price is $99.99 paid once per year, not $8.33 charged each month. Review the billing interval, renewal terms, and final total in your account before confirming a paid subscription.

Does creating an account start the VPN?

No. Download and install the compatible app, then connect inside it. Visiting the website does not protect your device traffic.
